# Contribution Guidelines

Thanks for helping keep this list useful. By participating you agree to the [Code of Conduct](code-of-conduct.md).

## What belongs here

- Projects, libraries, skills, apps and guides that work with [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai) or its models.
- Open-source alternatives that replace a specific Higgsfield feature.
- The project must be public, working and maintained. Repositories with no commits for six months may be removed.

## What doesn't

- Tools that break Higgsfield's terms of service: shared or auto-created accounts, session hijacking, bypassing paid limits.
- Mirrors of other people's content, empty repositories and pure link farms.
- Paid products without a free tier or open source code, unless they are official.

## Format

- One pull request per addition.
- Add the entry at the bottom of the matching section.
- Use `- [Name](link) - Description.`: the description starts with a capital letter, ends with a period and says what the project does, not how good it is.
- Link to the repository or the canonical page, not to a shortener, a referral link or a tracking URL.
- Check your change with [awesome-lint](https://github.com/sindresorhus/awesome-lint): `npx awesome-lint`.

Thank you for your suggestions!
